NAME
ReTwitterify Advanced

SUGGESTED SLUG
retwitterify-advanced

SUMMARY
Restore Twitter branding on X: blue birds, Twitter headers and tab titles, plus familiar Tweet and Retweet labels.

DESCRIPTION
Make X feel more like Twitter again.

ReTwitterify Advanced restores Twitter branding across the English X web interface:

• Twitter in site headers, browser tab titles, navigation, and supported interface labels.
• Blue bird logos and browser tab icons, including the redesigned public login page.
• Tweet and Retweet wording for post and repost controls.
• Updates as interface content loads and changes.
• Separate switches for branding, bird logos, and terminology, plus a master pause switch.

The extension is designed to leave tweet bodies, recognized profile/name fields, direct-message content, and text you are writing unchanged. Website addresses and link destinations remain unchanged. X’s experiments or future interface changes may require additional updates.

Everything runs locally. ReTwitterify Advanced stores only four appearance preferences in your browser and makes no analytics or telemetry requests. After installing, refresh your open X tabs.

Works on x.com and twitter.com, including their www and mobile subdomains. English interface wording is supported. Other websites and embedded tweets are outside its scope.

Inspired by Xenoreaper’s ReTwitterify, with a new replacement engine, expanded wording coverage, live settings, and support for newer logos. Bird geometry is adapted under the Mozilla Public License 2.0.
Original project: https://github.com/Xenoreaper/ReTwitterify

Independent project. Not affiliated with X or Twitter.
